One mark per scheduled run, placed at its actual run time, so several runs in a day sit close together and a pause shows as a gap. The shaded band is ±1.96×SE of the Trim-25 ensemble, where SE is the dispersion of its in-sample one-quarter-ahead fits (≈pp). It is computed on the same final-vintage data the models were fitted to, so read it as fit dispersion, not as a 95% prediction interval.
