Method in one paragraph

Quarterly GDP growth is nowcast from monthly indicators by 21 models under two ragged-edge treatments: Bridged (missing months of the target quarter are ARIMA-imputed before estimation — the classic bridge treatment) and Real-time (each specification is built from exactly the indicator months observed at run time; state-space models absorb the edge with the Kalman filter). Ensembles aggregate the single models by mean, median, trimmed mean of the best quartile, and best single model, re-ranked under each run's information set.
