Method

Quarterly GDP growth is nowcast from monthly indicators by 21 models under two ragged-edge treatments. Bridged imputes the target quarter's missing months with an ARIMA before estimation — the classic bridge treatment. Real-time builds each specification from exactly the indicator months observed at run time, with the state-space models absorbing the edge through the Kalman filter. The four ensembles — mean, median, trimmed mean of the best quartile by RMSE, and the single best model — are re-ranked under each run's information set. A nowcast only moves when the indicators feeding it move, so a flat stretch between releases is the tracker working, not failing. Two versions. The blue figure is V2 Wide, the current suite: the same 21 methods on a 7–9 indicator joint baseline, plus single-indicator models, BIC screening and CEIC extras; Trim-25 headline. The orange line is V1 Core, the September 2026 suite, frozen: 21 models on a two-indicator baseline (Korea and India: industrial production + PMI; Indonesia: vehicle sales + PMI), Trim-25 headline. Both run on the same data at every run; each country page keeps their real-time record against the GDP prints, and the backcast exercise replays both over past quarters on today's release pattern (an approximation, kept on its own page).
